Mappy

Welcome to Mappy!


Mappy is a web-based concept mapping tool. Mappy will generate word clouds based on your input, including written text, pictures, and speech. You can personalize your map to be more helpful for you by adding new words, deleting existing ones, and organizing them in a way that makes sense to you. Mappy will also help you visualize relationships between words and provide definitions, pictures, and a pronunciation guide if needed. Sign in below or create an account to get started :)
